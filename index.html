About Us

Patient-Focused Healthcare You Can Trust

Priyam Hospital, located on Katol Bypass Road in Dabha, Nagpur, is a dedicated healthcare facility providing specialized medical services in two critical areas — Advanced Eye Care and Maternity & Women's Healthcare.

Known online as Priyam Eye & Maternity Hospital, the hospital focuses on patient care in a clean and comfortable environment.

Conveniently situated near White House and Galaxy Hospital on Katol Bypass Road, it serves families across Dabha and greater Nagpur.
