About Us

Patient-Focused Healthcare You Can Trust

Priyam Hospital, located on Katol Bypass Road in Dabha, Nagpur, is a dedicated healthcare facility providing specialized medical services in two critical areas — Advanced Eye Care and Maternity & Women's Healthcare.

Known online as Priyam Eye & Maternity Hospital, we are committed to delivering compassionate, evidence-based care in a clean and comfortable environment. Our focus is on the individual patient — ensuring every visit is met with attention, respect, and quality treatment.

Conveniently situated near White House and Galaxy Hospital on Katol Bypass Road, we serve families across Dabha and greater Nagpur with accessible, specialized healthcare.
